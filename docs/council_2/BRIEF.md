## Where the project stands (read the sources; do not trust this summary blindly)
Project: a farm of Topstep 150K Combines and Express Funded Accounts (XFA) on NQ micro futures. Strategies ("sleeves"): `nq_noise` (noise-area momentum), `nq_vwap` (VWAP+ATR), `nq_gap30` (gap + first-30-min filtered VWAP). The default farm trades 3 noise-area + 2 gap-VWAP funded slots, one Combine open at a time, Combine size round(9,600 / ATR$) micros (R = 9,600 per ATR), funded size 1.2 x cushion/ATR before the first payout (at a $2,000 balance) and 0.8 after, flat by 15:55 ET, entries from 09:30 ET, cost $1.22 + 1 tick = $1.72 per micro round trip. Combine: $9,000 target, $4,500 trailing loss limit that locks at $0, soft daily loss limit $3,000, best day below 55% of the target, 150 micros, fee $199 per 21-session billing cycle.
Data: Databento NQ 1-minute 2010-06 to 2026-07-02 (repaired every-session copy `/home/user/data_raw_live`, set `TOPSTEP_RAW_DIR=/home/user/data_raw_live`). Dev = up to 2022-12-31; hold-out 2023-01 to 2026-07 (NOT clean: used many times). LSE (live feed vendor) no longer serves more futures history, only live ticks; the 75 post-July-2026 sessions we have are INVALID (24% of sessions have holes).

Established so far (sections of `FINDINGS.md`, `docs/TRUST_LEDGER.md`, `docs/COUNCIL_1.md`):
* Mechanics (signals, engine, evaluator, cost re-basing) were independently reproduced; the dollar forecasts are not trustworthy. Dev = about 4-5 independent 3-year windows; starts in 2016-2018 carry most profit. As modelled the baseline's 3-year farm is mean $12.7k / median $2.9k; on today's cost-to-ATR ("re-based exact") $38.8k / $22.8k with 87% positive (needs the percentage edge to be price-independent).
* Hold-out: farms started 2023-01 to 2024-06 did well in the real cost era (medians $14.5k over 378 sessions, $16.3k over 504). **Every farm started from December 2024 to July 2025 (16 starts, about one independent year) lost $4.2-4.6k in fees with no payout** (FINDINGS 35). Combines: noise-area passed 0 of 42 for 2025 H1 starts and 9% in 2025 H2 (19-42% in earlier halves); gap-VWAP 7%; 16.4 Combines bought per year, 2.1 passed. The 55% consistency rule blocked every 2025 H1 Combine that reached the target (6 of 42) and 4 of 44 in 2025 H2.
* Sleeves since 2024-07 (net R = P&L/ATR): VWAP +16.3 R (not in the farm; weakest dev evidence), noise-area +9.2, gap-VWAP +5.9; last 12 months noise-area -1.6 R, VWAP +4.8, gap +2.6; the top-5 days equal 100% of noise-area's profit since mid-2024; leader changes every half year; daily R correlations 0.48-0.74.
* A zero-edge coin flip (random side each day, same costs and sizing) passes the Combine 13.6% in dev, 15.3% for 2023-24 starts, 16.2% for starts since 2024-12 (real: 6.5% noise-area, 9.7% gap-VWAP; not significantly different, about 23 independent Combines). Aggressive sizing does NOT raise pass odds (coin flip 13.6% -> 10.8% -> 9.3% at 1x/2x/4x R); the pass probability of a driftless process is nearly scale-free (about 18% in the continuous limit). A coin flip's EV per try is negative (-$65 to -$113 in dev) because its funded accounts are paid only ~30% of the time (FINDINGS 36, `run_zero_edge.py`).
* Regime prediction: no predictive power found (trailing variance ratio, autocorrelation, range; FINDINGS 5); 9 causal switcher variants (strategy choice and size by trailing R) beat no fixed sleeve in dev or hold-out (`run_regime_switch.py`, FINDINGS 36). Splits and settings (a1, a2, first-payout balance, Combine R) do not rescue the post-Dec-2024 farms (FINDINGS 35). ~5,000 configurations were tried in the project (heavy selection bias).
* Forward test (PR18, `docs/PREREGISTRATION_18.md`): R-unit kill rules, weekly looks from session 60, can only STOP never confirm (stops 23.4% of full-edge and 66.4% of zero-edge paths by session 250; likelihood ratio 2.8). The live harness (`python -m paper.run --source ticks ...`, `paper/`) is built and parity-tested against the back-test but never run on the real tick stream (tick field names and volume basis unverified). It needs an always-on machine; a viewer (Vercel + Supabase) exists but is parked.
