# Pre-registration 14 (committed BEFORE the run): is there a setting with > 80% of funded accounts paid AND a first payout within 90 calendar days?

User question: "Is it possible to search for a setting with accounts that paid > 80% and days to pass > 90 cal days?" Read as: accounts paid > 80% and calendar days from buying the Combine to the first payout under 90 (the goal the user has held since "<90 cal days would be the goal"); the literal reading (over 90 days) is already answered by the earlier grids (17 cells with >= 80% paid and a median above 90 days; best mean farm $21.4k: start 0.4 -> 1.2 at +$750 -> 2.0 at +$1,250, 80.2% paid, 206 days). Known before running (sections 22-25): no earlier cell has paid >= 75% with a median <= 100 days; the best paid share with a median <= 90 days is 70% (step-down ladders, 27-37% of the profit); the closest earlier cell is the two-tier setting (noise-area as today with first payout $1,000, gap-VWAP at size 0.4): 80.9% paid but 104 days.

## What can move the days
The days from Combine start to the first payout are: pass (median 28 days) + activation to payout. The payout needs a balance >= the first-payout balance t1 AND 5 winning days of >= $150 (about 26 sessions, i.e. about 36 calendar days at the least). So the levers are the size before the first payout (a1, speed of reaching t1 against the risk of dying first) and t1 itself; the two groups of accounts (noise-area x3, gap-VWAP x2) behave very differently and may get different values. Unchanged: Combine R = 9,600, a2 = 0.8, first-payout share 50%, one Combine open at a time, costs, RTH-only, dev-only rule.

## Search space (declared)
Constant sizes per group (no scaling): noise-area group a1 in {0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.6} x t1 in {$750, $1,000, $1,500, $2,000} (20 settings); gap-VWAP group a1 in {0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.8, 1.2} x t1 in {$750, $1,000, $1,500, $2,000} (24 settings). All 20 x 24 = 480 combinations are evaluated at ACCOUNT level (cheap): the single-sleeve results (Combine starts every 3 sessions 2010-2022, Combine outcomes computed once per sleeve) are pooled noise x3 + gap x2 exactly as in sections 22-25. Farm-level metrics (ten 3-year dev farms, 2011-2020 starts, one Combine open at a time) are computed only for combinations that qualify at account level (at most the 30 with the highest pooled paid share).

## Qualifying and ranking
Qualifying (account level, 2010-2022 starts): pooled share of funded accounts ever paid >= 80% AND median calendar days Combine start -> first payout (accounts that are paid) <= 90. Because the extremes of 480 correlated cells are picked, a qualifying combination must also pass the stability checks: in BOTH halves of the starts (2010-2016 and 2017-2022) paid >= 75% and median days <= 100; on the 2023+ starts paid >= 70% and median days <= 100 (these years have been seen, reported as a check, not as a fresh test). Among combinations that pass everything, the highest dev mean farm is the recommendation; its p25, worst farm, green years, deaths, payouts and seen farm starts are reported next to the baseline's (today: paid 61.5%, 78 days, mean farm $17.5k, p25 -$1.6k, worst -$12.4k), and the edge-removed placebo is run for it.
If nothing qualifies: report the frontier (the highest paid share with days <= 90, and the lowest median days with paid >= 80%) and the closest combination. If something qualifies, the answer is NOT an adoption: it is a candidate for a forward test (480 looks on overlapping dev windows).

## Honest limits
Paid shares have a sampling error of about +-3 points (about 400 funded accounts per cell on overlapping starts), so 80-83% is "about 80%". The pooled share is a mix: the two gap-VWAP accounts are paid almost always at small size, the three noise-area accounts (where the profit is) usually are not; the per-group paid shares are reported next to the pooled one. Accounts paid and speed are both measured on funded accounts of the sequential-start sample (not the farm), as in sections 22-25; the farm-level paid share (unfinished accounts counted as unpaid) is lower.
