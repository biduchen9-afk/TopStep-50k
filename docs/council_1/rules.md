## Ranked recommendations
1. Send one written support email (RTP trigger, "account stacking" vs deliberate Combine recycling, hedging with mixed strategies, Live call-up and automation, bots on XFAs, VPS) and keep the reply. It costs nothing and covers the items that can move the median by more than any parameter (5c: -$6k median, 6: -$7k mean).
2. Measure the liquidation overshoot (row 1 experiment, plus a log of the first 20 real DLL days) and replace the fixed-$ slip by ticks x contracts in the stress table. Pre-commit: average >= 3 ticks/contract (about $130/event) means median < 0, do not scale.
3. Add to the stress table: Consistency-forced scenario, max payouts per account (3 and 5), API fee, and report medians/p25, not means. Run the forward test small and staggered (1-2 accounts, Combine starts spread out), payouts by Aeropay.

## What NOT to do
* Do not quote the $12.7k mean as an expectation (median $2.9k; path noise +-$4-6k).
* Do not tune a tighter bot-side daily stop on this data: -$2,700 / -$2,500 give median -$0.4k / $1.3k at zero slip (worse than $2.9k) and mean +$4-6k but median still negative at $250 slip [run].
* Do not buy several correlated Combines the same day, hold opposite NQ positions across accounts, or use any API on a Live account.
* Do not take rules from [3P] blogs (DLL removal, no news rule, VPS fine); do not bank the doubled payout cap.
* Do not read the FOMC result as a free gain (in sample, 125 days, dates from memory).

## Biggest thing the lead may be wrong about
That the remaining risk is parametric (fill, ladder, news) and the farm is rule-clean. Topstep's written, discretionary conduct rules (RTP on multi-account MLL days, account stacking, Live call-up closing all XFAs, no bots on Live) describe this farm's shape, and the engine's own replays trip the first one in most 3-year paths; the forced Consistency path alone turns +$12.7k / $2.9k into -$0.9k / -$3.1k.
