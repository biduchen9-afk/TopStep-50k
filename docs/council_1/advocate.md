# Devil's advocate / go-no-go (council seat)
**Verdict: NO-GO on "the farm as an investment" (5 accounts, scaling, more firms). Conditional GO only on a capped (<= $1.5k) LIVE learning experiment whose purpose is fills and rules, not proving edge.** My credence (judgement, not computed): live 3-year mean > 0 after frictions ~45%; median >= the user's $2k bar ~30%.
Method: reproduced the baseline (247 farms, repaired data, rules v2: mean $12,719, median $2,859) and re-ran it under stresses (code and csv in this folder: stress.py, seen.py, power2.py, acct2.py, farm_*.csv). Slippage runs rebuild the sleeves with the new cost. Topstep rules are still second-hand (sandbox cannot open help.topstep.com).

## 1. Why EV is not demonstrably positive
- **Four independent windows, not 247.** Non-overlapping 3-yr farms: +$2.9k (2010), -$6.2k (2013), +$52.4k (2016), +$3.3k (2019). Farms started 2016-18 are 31% of the 247 and carry 99% of total net; the 76 farms started 2012-14 are ALL negative (mean -$6.9k: fees $15.4k, payouts $8.6k). Excluding 2016-17 starts: mean $3.3k, median -$0.3k, 49% positive. Year-block SE of the $12.7k mean: $6.2k (11 blocks) to ~$10k (4 independent).
- **Account level, same story.** Combine starts in 2017-18 (20% of starts) give 99% of the noise-area EV and 90% of the gap-VWAP EV. Without them the farm-mix rate falls $29.3 -> $1.7 per Combine session ($22.1k -> $1.3k per mature 3 years); without 2018 alone $11.8. Per noise-area Combine start: P(EV>0) 11%, median -$199, top 1% of starts = 66% of total EV. "Steady-state $22k" is one year of volatility.
- **Edge evidence is thin where the money is.** Noise-area (76% of Combine time, 82% of funded accounts) dev t = 1.64 (Sharpe 0.46 per ATR unit). gap30 dev t = 3.46 but picked from 156 variants and found after seeing the 2025 failure. ~6,000 looks (4,752 grid + 156 + 300 + 104 + 144 + 185 + 455) all on the same 12.5 years; hold-out never clean; a June 2026 replication reports OOS Sharpe 0.39 for noise-area (FINDINGS 17). 2025+: Sharpe 0.28 / 0.38, t 0.35 / 0.47.
- **Break-even edge ~50% of the observed one.** Scaling the dev-fitted edge: 75% -> mean $5.3k / median $1.8k; 50% -> -$0.8k / -$3.8k (40% positive); 25% -> -$4.2k / -$5.0k. Winner's-curse shrinkage of 40-50% is not exotic after 6,000 looks.
- **Friction knife-edges (mean / median / % positive, all 247 farms).** As modelled $12.7k / $2.9k / 60%. +1 tick round-trip slippage $8.7k / $1.2k / 51%; +2 ticks -$0.5k / -$7.8k / 39%. Liquidation $100 beyond the DLL $13.6k / $1.8k; $250 $2.1k / -$3.2k / 43%. Price $229 $10.7k / $0.7k. **"Mild" (+1 tick, +$100): $0.6k / -$3.9k / 40%. "Harsh" (+1 tick, +$250, $229): -$2.8k / -$6.7k / 35%.** Leverage: +2 ticks cost only 20% of the noise-area sleeve edge ($4.45 -> $3.58 per micro-day) yet move the farm median by -$10.7k.
- **Why live fills are worse than the back-test:** signal at bar close, fill at next open is an adverse-selection-free fiction for momentum entries (price is already moving); stops fill exactly at the stop (audit: +1 tick/stop = 2-6% of sleeve P&L); thin pre-roll sessions; Tier-1 news cap (9 micros +-5 min) not modelled; DLL liquidation fill undocumented; one-session activation lag cuts the farm paid share to 46.8% and lifts median days to payout to ~194; payout processing, platform/API/data fees not modelled; automation must run on a personal device (VPS reportedly banned; sessions run 20:30-02:55 Cambodia time).
- **Rule/counterparty risk.** One missing payout rule (positive net profit since last payout) moved the mean by $3.5k; XFA ladder unresolved; Topstep changed payout terms in 2026. The design sizes funded accounts at 3-4x Kelly on purpose and lets them die, which is the profile the Responsible Trading Program / Risk Adjustments pages target (forces consistency path: 47% paid, $6k cap). Five accounts on one signal die together (payouts in only 17-26% of months in FINDINGS 5/16). The 3+2 split runs two different sleeves at once: opposite NQ positions across own accounts could be read as cross-account hedging (banned; unverified, not quantified). All-noise x5 avoids it but is worse (mean $7.5k, median $1.6k, p25 -$9.9k).
- **The present regime is already bleeding.** Farm from 2025-01-02: 388 sessions, 24 Combines, 3 funded, 0 paid, -$6.8k (fees only); from 2025-07-01: -$4.8k (18 Combines, 0 paid); from 2026-01-02: -$3.4k in 130 sessions (about $6.6k/yr burn). At 378 sessions the dev p10 is -$6.5k (all starts) and -$3.5k (2016+ starts): worse than the 10th percentile of the favourable era. Costs are only 0.2% of ATR now (vs 1.3-2.6% in 2010-17), so this is edge decay, not cost.

## 2. What the user can lose (baseline farm, one Combine at a time)
- Burn: fees $13.7k per 3 years = **$4.6k/yr ($381/month, 18 Combines/yr)**, continuing in a dead regime. Cash after 12 months: median -$1.5k, 61% below zero, p5 -$5.4k; after 24 months still 50% <= -$0.1k.
- Peak cumulative outlay (min cash): median -$4.7k, p10 -$10.3k, worst -$11.6k; P(dip < -$5k) 47%, < -$8k 23%, < -$10k 12%. Mild friction: p10 -$11.3k, worst -$15.0k. Harsh: p10 -$16.1k, worst -$17.6k. No floor except the user's own stop (2025-26 farms have no payout in 1.5 years).
- 3-year net: P(> $10k) 40% as modelled, 24% mild, 8% harsh; P(< -$5k) 24% / 42% / 53%.
- **J-curve vs. a hard cap.** A -$3k stop is hit by 66% of dev farms and 29% of those ended above +$10k; the stop keeps mean $6.7k but median -$3k and 30% positive (-$5k stop: hit 47%, mean $8.7k). A tight cap and the lottery payoff are in tension: whoever wants the right tail needs ~$10-12k of risk capital and 2-3 years of patience; a $1.5k budget buys information, not a payout.
- **Size of the experiment:** Stage 1 <= $1.5k total (about 6 Combine starts at the model's $255 average; ~77% chance of at least one pass), one Combine at a time, no top-ups after the cap. Stage 2 (one funded account, still no farm) <= $5k cumulative only after the gates in 3. Nothing above ~$10k before 24 months of live evidence. Money must be money whose loss changes nothing.

## 3. Kill criteria and minimum evidence (PR15 reviewed)
- **PR15 cannot confirm anything.** Power sim (block bootstrap of 2023-26 pooled 3 noise + 2 gap micros, current dollar scale): P(both kill rules pass at 120 sessions) = 87% at dev edge, 77% at half edge, **64% at zero edge, 52% at an edge of -50%**; P(cum P&L > 0 at 250 sessions) 77% / 63% / 47%. "Not killed" is a likelihood ratio of ~1.4. Sharpe 0.5 vs 0 needs ~11 years for t > 1.64 (0.8: 4.2 years). The PR says so (farm not scored) but still labels it a test; it has no GO rule and no action table.
- **Rule (ii) is unit-ambiguous.** In raw dollars the dev 5th percentile is only -$1.2k (60 sessions) / -$1.3k (120) because the dollar scale has doubled (daily SD of the pooled position $342 dev vs $754 now): the rule then fires in 27% of windows at the seen-period edge, 37% at dev edge, 58% at zero edge (a coin flip). Normalised to ATR (about -$9.7k / -$11.3k at today's ATR) it fires 1-2% at dev edge and only 7% at zero edge (it almost never fires). Rule (iii) (both sleeves negative at 120): 12-13% with edge, 35% at zero.
- **What I would change:** (a) state thresholds in ATR units, not dollars; (b) add fidelity gates that are decisive in weeks, not years: trade-level parity vs back-test on the same bars (entry/exit within 1 tick, >= 98% of sessions), realised cost per micro round trip vs model $1.72; (c) add explicit GO/STOP actions; (d) start the clock now: Jul-Oct 2026 bars already exist, do not wait for the LSE feed (its futures class is labelled eurex, NQ may be absent); (e) paper has no real fills, so add 1 live MNQ (any broker) running the frozen sleeves, >= 100 fills (~5 months, SE of mean slippage ~0.1 tick).
- **Gates before real money beyond the first account (all required):** G1 Topstep text read first-hand plus written support answers on automation from a personal device, trade copier, positions in several own accounts, DLL liquidation fill, news cap. G2 >= 100 real fills with mean cost <= model + 1 tick ($2.22 per micro RT; STOP above +2 ticks, where the model's sign flips). G3 one full Combine -> XFA -> payout cycle received, arithmetic equal to the engine. G4 parity G above. G5 >= 250 sessions with cumulative ATR-normalised pooled P&L >= 0 (weak: a zero-edge strategy passes 47%; it only guards against clearly negative), then judge scaling on option economics (fees are capped), not on proof of edge.
- **Kill:** any adverse G1 answer; cost > +2 ticks; cumulative net outlay reaches the stage cap; 250 sessions with ATR-normalised pooled P&L below the 5th percentile of dev windows (-14.5 R at 60 sessions, -16.8 R at 120); data-validity failure. No averaging down.

## 4. Strongest counter-arguments (fair)
- Payoff is bounded below by fees and unbounded above; even my "mild" row has 24% of farms above +$10k. Edge-removed placebos lose in every test, so profit comes from the edge, not a rule artefact; five audits found no look-ahead; the project corrected its own numbers down repeatedly.
- **The pre-2016 failures are largely a cost-scale artefact.** Per-micro cost was 1.3-2.6% of ATR in 2010-17 (80-150 micros, the 150 cap binding in 2010-13) vs 0.17-0.37% since 2020 (10-22 micros). Farms from 2016+ starts (105): mean $32.0k, median $27.1k, 96% positive; under "mild" friction still $10.4k / $10.2k / 92%, "harsh" $7.7k / $7.1k / 82%. A colleague's scratch re-costing at today's cost/ATR (recost output in this folder, not checked by me) shows a farm mean of ~$32k and 95% positive. If so, down-weighting 2011-14 is right.
- Seen farms started 2023-01 to 2025-12 (every 10 sessions, 1-year horizon, n=65): mean $3.8k, median $3.3k, 65% positive vs dev 2016+ mean $4.7k, median -$0.2k, 50%: no decay through 2024. Noise-area is the least-tuned sleeve (published rule) and its seen-period t is 2.2 (dev+seen ~2.7).
- Unknowns that flip the sign (fills, DLL, rules) are cheap to measure with a $199-1.5k live test; waiting only adds the cost of lost time. The user explicitly accepts lottery risk.

## 5. Recommendations
1. **Re-frame and cap:** the median farm (+$2.9k, -$3.9k under mild friction) does NOT meet the user's "$2k in 3 years is unacceptable" bar; only the right tail does. Say so, fix a Stage-1 budget of $1.5k and the stop rules in 3 before any purchase.
2. **Measure what flips the sign, with real fills:** 1 live MNQ with the frozen sleeves in parallel with the paper run, and read the Topstep pages first-hand (allow the two hosts) plus support emails. Rewrite PR15 as above and start the clock on existing Jul-Oct 2026 data.
3. **Remove the DLL-fill dependence in the design:** test the existing self-imposed daily breaker (FINDINGS 18: -$1.5k breaker improved p25 and worst farm on the old data) on the repaired data under DLL slip $0/$100/$250 and +1 tick, as a pre-registered single look, so Topstep's -$3k liquidation is never the exit.
- **Do NOT:** run 5 accounts or two Combines at once (exploratory: p25 -$7.7k, worst -$16.2k); add other firms (variance, bans); re-tune or adopt the S1 gap winner (z 2.16) or the 2+3 / gap x5 splits (seen-informed); judge the test by farm net or 3-year medians; top up after the cap; treat 2023-24 as confirmation; use a VPS; start before G1.
- **Biggest thing the lead may be wrong about:** treating regime/edge significance as THE open question that the paper forward test will answer. Edge cannot be resolved in under ~10 years; the sign of the farm is decided by fill quality (+1 to +2 ticks), the DLL liquidation fill (+$100 to +$250), price/activation/news rules, none of which a next-open paper test can observe, and PR15 would pass a zero-edge strategy 64% of the time. The decision-relevant uncertainty is cheap and live; the expensive one is not decision-relevant.
- **Evidence that would change my mind:** G1-G4 passed with measured cost <= +1 tick; an ATR-normalised, pre-registered mild-friction row with positive median; one real payout; 250 sessions in a >= 2016-like volatility regime with both sleeves positive.
