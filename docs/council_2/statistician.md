2. **Decide real money by loss budget, not significance.** A half edge (EV about +$6k a year) never confirms; a dead edge costs about $1.4k a year in expectation (2025 farms: $4.2-4.6k fees, read). One first Combine passes only 13-27%: a lottery ticket, not a test.

**What not to do.** Treat no-STOP as confirmation; count overlapped starts as N; infer "dead" from missing a 25-30% pass rate; test Dec 2024 or "weakest window" as hypotheses; stack SPRT as a second kill on PR18 (false-kill about 32%); treat the hold-out as confirmation.

**Biggest thing the chair may be wrong about.** That a year of paper trading, or a pass-rate comparison with the coin flip, separates alive from dead. A year yields a kill signal with likelihood ratio about 10 and never a confirmation; "not 25%" does not mean dead. Equally wrong: reading the post-Dec-2024 failure as decay.
