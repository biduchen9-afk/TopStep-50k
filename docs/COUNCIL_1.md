# Council 1: minutes (15 October 2026)

Question put to the council: (1) is 3 noise-area + 2 gap-VWAP still the best configuration without overfitting; (2) what new research should come next; (3) how should the project proceed. Five seats worked independently on the repo (audited baseline, FINDINGS 20-28b, PR16): statistician, Topstep-rules analyst, strategy researcher, farm architect, devil's advocate. Their full memos are in `docs/council_1/`. Every number below is a seat's own computation unless stated; the chair re-ran two of them (listed at the end). Nothing was selected or adopted in this meeting.

## 1. Where the council agrees
1. **The dev evidence is about 4 to 5 independent draws, not 247 farms.** Farm nets have an autocorrelation time of 49 to 57 starts (N_eff 5.5); 2012-14 starts lose in 76 of 76 farms (mean -$6.9k), 2015-19 starts win in 121 of 129 (mean $27.8k); starts in 2016-18 carry 99% of the summed net. Farm differences under about $5k are ties. A rank of 3+2 against 2+3 against gap x5 cannot be established on this data (statistician: block-bootstrap z 1.3-1.4, minimum detectable difference about $10-11k at 80% power).
2. **No more dev grids** (sizing, triggers, filters, volatility/ADX gates, trailing-performance switches): ~5,000 configurations have been tried over the project, cell gains in one dev half correlate only 0.04-0.19 with the other half, and the PR16 gates alone would pass on null data 29-34% of the time.
3. **Three things outside the strategies' edge decide the sign of the result:** (a) the cost basis (below), (b) how Topstep fills a daily-loss-limit liquidation, (c) Topstep's discretionary conduct rules. A fourth, smaller one: price ($229 instead of $199 cuts the median to about $0.7k).
4. **The forward test as written (PR15) cannot confirm anything** and needs a rewrite: thresholds in ATR units (the dollar version fires in 27-58% of windows even with the full edge), decisive gates that need weeks (trade-level parity with the back-test, realised cost per micro against the model's $1.72), and a statement that it can only kill (80% power needs ~6.6 years for gap-VWAP and ~29 for noise-area).
5. **Do not scale now** (concurrency, more slots, more firms, other assets): it multiplies the same exposure. No structural lever delivers a first payout inside 90 days (26% of baseline farms get one).
6. **Report and compare differently:** medians and the bad quartile first, the mean last (the top 10% of farms hold 54% of the summed net); start-year blocks as the unit; cohort net (every payout of the Combines bought in the window) next to the 3-year cut-off, because the cut-off flips signs (gap x5 against 3+2: -$1.3k realised, -$10.9k cohort).

## 2. The finding that changes the picture (strategy seat; chair re-ran it)
The model charges a fixed $1.72 per micro round trip. As a share of ATR14 that is 2.5% in 2013, 1.3% in 2015-16, 0.35% in 2020-24 and 0.2% in 2025-26, because NQ rose roughly 15-fold. So the weak 2011-16 years in the dev sample are largely a cost-to-risk artefact, and the "regime dependence" and the 2017-18 concentration are mostly that artefact plus ordinary luck in a convex farm (day-shuffle nulls reproduce the start-year dispersion with P = 0.2-0.45; variance ratios 0.64-0.97, no persistence). Charging costs at today's cost-to-ATR with the same percentage moves, the 3+2 baseline over the 247 dev farms goes from mean $12.7k / median $2.9k / p25 -$4.7k / 60% positive to **mean $32.3k / median $21.4k / p25 $14.6k / 95% positive** (2012-14 starts turn positive). Gross Sharpe is 1.28 (dev) and 1.31 (2023+) for noise-area, 1.39 and 0.92 for gap-VWAP. **Caveats:** it is a counterfactual that needs the percentage edge to be price-independent and 1-tick slippage to hold at today's price; the farm that started in 2025-01 is -$6.8k either way, and the last 18 months of re-costed Sharpe (0.43, z -1.1, the weakest of 10 windows) cannot be separated from bad luck yet. The re-costing also reverses the advocate's friction stresses: +1 or +2 ticks look fatal in the dev costing only because the early years were already cost-dominated.

## 3. Positions and disagreements
* **Is the baseline understated or a coin flip?** Strategy seat: understated ($21k median re-costed), real risk is decay. Advocate (a judgement, not a computation): mean above zero about 45%, median at or above the user's $2k bar about 30%, no-go as an investment, conditional go only for a capped learning experiment. Statistician: the edge is real in aggregate (1.7% chance of the baseline mean in zero-edge worlds) but which sleeve is real is unresolved: gap-VWAP t 3.46 but picked from 156 variants (deflated 0.57-0.82), noise-area t 1.64 (0.59 without 2017-18) yet it holds 76% of Combine time and 83% of funded accounts. These positions are compatible: the cost re-basing is the cheap way to find out how much of the gap is measurement.
* **Is 3+2 best?** Statistician: no evidence either way; 3+2 is never the minimax-regret split (2+3 on mean and median, gap x5 on p25), and the burden of proof is asymmetric (incumbent exempt). Farm seat: rank by the worst case across daily-loss-limit slips $0/$100/$250: 3+2 falls to $2.1k at $250, 2+3 and gap x5 to about $10k, but gap x5 lost $8.3k on the 2023 start and pays out later. Advocate: adopt none. Agreed handling: keep 3+2 as a labelled default; test a frozen menu of exactly three (3+2, 2+3, gap x5), stated expectation "cannot tell" at 70% or more.
* **Concurrency.** Farm seat: it is the dominant structural lever and pure leverage (5 open Combines: mean $31.0k, median $17.9k, worst -$21.8k, bad windows lose 2.2x more, the 2025 start -$15.2k). Advocate and farm seat both say not now. Two Combines at once gave +$3.8k [-0.3, +8.1] with twice the cash-risk tail. Left as a risk-appetite decision for the user, after the fill and rule unknowns are settled.
* **The liquidation stress.** The advocate's $250 beyond the limit is about 5.9 ticks per contract at the farm's ~85 contracts and ~75 liquidations per farm. Rules seat re-expressed it in ticks per contract: 1 tick gives mean $7.4k / median $2.3k, 2 ticks $11.6k / $2.3k, at 3 ticks the median turns negative. Topstep says only that slippage may push the fill slightly beyond the limit. The user can measure it on a real account.
* **Conduct rules (rules seat, first-party text via search, unverified directly):** a forced Consistency path for all accounts turns the baseline into mean -$0.9k / median -$3.1k and paid share 21% against 48%; the Responsible Trading Program lists several accounts hitting the loss limit in one day as a placement signal (77% of 3-year farms have such a day with funded accounts alone); account stacking and opposite positions across accounts are prohibited; bots are allowed via API (about $14.50-29 per month), not on Live accounts; the Risk Team can call an account up to Live and all XFAs close (one blog says after five payouts; capping payouts at 5 per account gives mean $11.4k, at 3 $5.7k). These are discretionary and describe the farm's shape.
* **Capital (farm seat, baseline one-at-a-time, 247 farms):** peak cash at risk median $4.7k, p90 $10.3k, max $11.6k; P(cash line ever below -$2k / -$5k / -$10k) 78% / 47% / 12%; first payout (calendar days) p25 / p50 / p75 / p90 = 89 / 169 / 302 / 432. Suggested loss budget: $5k for a pilot, $12k for the full one-at-a-time farm, $20k or more for five at once. The numbers are about five draws of one regime switch, not probabilities.

## 4. Recommended plan (for the user to approve or change)
**Phase 0, no compute, user actions.** (a) Start a new session on this branch now that the Topstep hosts are allowed and have it read the help-centre pages first-hand (`docs/NEXT_SESSION.md`). (b) Send one written support question and keep the reply: how a liquidation by the daily or maximum loss limit fills; what triggers the Responsible Trading Program (several accounts hitting the loss limit on one day); whether several accounts of one person running the same signal is "account stacking"; mixed-strategy hedging across accounts; when an XFA is called up to Live; bots and VPS use; the 150K XFA scaling row; whether 10:00 ET releases are Tier 1 events. (c) Optional, cheap: measure the liquidation overshoot on an existing Combine with a Personal daily loss limit of $100.
**Phase 1, PR17 (foundation fix, zero new configurations).** Re-base the dev evaluation at today's cost-to-ATR with the scaling rule frozen beforehand (reference price = mean of 2024-26; costs at 1, 2 and 3 ticks); restate the liquidation stress in ticks per contract; add the rules scenarios (forced Consistency path, payouts capped per account, API fee, Tier 1 FOMC windows); report cohort net beside the 3-year cut-off and start-year blocks as the unit; re-measure the baseline range. Then the statistician's frozen-menu protocol for 3+2 against 2+3 against gap x5: year-block / regime-resampled bootstrap in a zero-edge world, switch only if P(better) is at least 0.975 on the mean, at least 0.80 on the median and p25, the gain is at least $5k, it is non-inferior without 2017-18, and it beats the null's 95th percentile; otherwise keep 3+2.
**Phase 2, forward test v2 (PR15 rewrite).** ATR-unit kill rules; decisive gates on parity and measured cost per micro; start the clock on whatever bars exist once the data feed is reachable. A real-money fills pilot (one MNQ with the frozen sleeves, 100 or more fills, total budget about $1.5k, or the farm architect's $5k seed-capped pilot) is the user's decision and is not started by the council.
**Phase 3, new research after Phase 1.** (D2) independent-sample validation of the two frozen sleeves with zero parameters on 2000-2010 QQQ/SPY and RTY/YM/ES (needs a data purchase; about 10 added independent years); (D3) trend-day capture at today's costs (12 configurations; about 21 trend days a year carry 193% / 131% of gross profit); D4 (frozen ports to crude, euro, bonds) and D5 (NQ-ES spread) only as watch items.
**Not now:** concurrency above one Combine, more slots or firms, permanent kill rules tuned on dev, trailing-performance gates, the Consistency path, ES ports for diversification (they lower the NQ portfolio Sharpe from 1.36 to 1.07), adopting the S1 gap cell or the frontier points, reading 2023-26 as confirmation, a VPS before the rules are confirmed in writing.
