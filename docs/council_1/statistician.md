# Statistician seat: can "3 noise + 2 gap" be shown best without overfitting?
All numbers recomputed 15 Oct 2026 on the repaired data, rules v2, dev (<= 2022) only; no repo file modified. Scripts and CSVs: `council/work/` (jobA = regime-resampled full-engine bootstrap, 180 draws; jobB = 432-cell account grids; an1-an8, lam, fa, wf_acct).

**Verdict.** The dev data can neither show that 3+2 is best nor that anything beats it. They are consistent with a real but modest edge (zero-edge resampled histories reproduce the baseline's $12.7k only 1.7% of the time), but every ranking of splits or sizings is below the noise floor, and what lean there is points gap-ward, not toward 3+2. The honest outcome of any test on this data is CANNOT TELL. Stop searching dev data.

## (a) What the data can and cannot support
1. **Effective sample.** The 247 farms are about 5 independent observations (integrated autocorrelation 49-57 starts, N_eff 4.3-5.1; only 4 non-overlapping 3-year windows exist). Calendar years are near-independent (lag-1 autocorrelation of yearly sleeve edge 0.0 / -0.07) so the unit is ~13 years, but two years carry the edge (top-2 years = 80% of the noise sleeve's summed yearly edge, 37% for gap). Regimes: one strong expansion (2017-18), one secondary (2020-22), five flat or negative years (2013-16, 2019). 128 of 247 farms touch 2017-18: 3+2 mean $24.8k, median $25.2k, 77% positive; the other 119: mean -$0.3k, median -$1.8k, 42% positive. 3+2 block means by start window 2010-13 / 13-16 / 16-19 / 19-20: -2.3k / +5.1k / +35.4k / +12.4k. 2+3 and gap x5 beat 3+2 in 52% / 54% of all (phase x independent-window) pairs: a coin flip.
2. **The weakest-evidence sleeve holds most of the farm.** Daily R = pnl/ATR, net. Noise-area: SR 0.46, t 1.64, year-block 90% CI of mean [-0.19, +1.89] x 0.01 ATR/day, t 0.59 without 2017-18, 69% of cumulative R in 2017-18. Gap-VWAP: SR 0.97, t 3.46 (yearly t 4.0, 11 of 13 years positive, t 2.5 without 2017-18). Deflated Sharpe (Bailey-Lopez de Prado, no skew term) at N = 100 / 1000 looks: gap 0.82 / 0.57, noise 0.18 / 0.05. The project's own ES port of the noise-area rule had dev Sharpe -0.22 / -0.11 / -0.21 (`results/es_gc_trials.csv`; ES gap-VWAP ports -0.17..+0.39). Seen 2023-26 (soft, contaminated): noise SR 1.16 but only +0.67 / +0.29 (x 0.01) in 2025 / 2026; gap 0.82. The sleeves correlate 0.61 daily, 0.75 yearly, so 3+2 diversifies little. Yet noise-area has 3 of 5 slots, 76% of Combine time, 83% of funded accounts.
3. **Detectable differences.** Treating 247 farms as iid gives SE $1.0k and z = 5.2 for (2+3) - (3+2); moving blocks of >= 26 starts give SE $3.6-4.1k, z 1.3-1.4 (gap x5: SE $3.0k, z -0.4). Minimum detectable (80% power, 5% two-sided = 2.8 SE): $10-11k for 2+3, $8.5k for gap x5, i.e. 70-85% of the baseline mean. Power to detect the observed +$5.1k is 27-33%; years of data needed at this regime mix: ~57 for +$5k, ~376 for +$2k. Account level: SE of a one-cell rate gain $7-12/session, MDE $20-33 = 46-77% of the gap rate ($43.4), 80-130% of the noise rate ($24.8). Gap minus noise rate: +$18.7, SE 12.6, z 1.5, 8 of 11 years. The baseline mean itself: $12.7k, 90% CI [-1.0k, +28k] (block L = 76).
4. **Full-engine regime bootstrap** (resample the 13 calendar years with replacement, rebuild every farm; 180 draws). 3+2: mean $14.8k, sd across histories $15.5k, 90% [-3.6k, +42.6k], P(mean > 0) 0.86, P(median > 0) 0.76; zero-edge world P(mean >= $12.7k) 1.7%, P(median >= $2.9k) 9%. 2+3 minus 3+2: mean +4.6k (P>0 0.87), median +3.4k (0.79), p25 +4.2k (0.87), positive share +14 pts (0.86). Gap x5 minus 3+2: mean +2.2k (sd 10.2k, 0.63), median +6.1k (0.80), p25 +7.1k (0.89), positive +19.6 pts (0.87). Nothing reaches 0.95, but the lean is gap-ward on every metric; the realised-path deficit of 2+3 in positive farms (48% vs 60%) sits in the 2010-13 start windows (1% of 2+3 farms positive) and is a path feature. (Each paired draw has sd $4.4k for 2+3, $10.2k for gap x5.)
5. **Selection accounting.** ~5,000 looks (4,752 + 185 + 455 + 144 + 104 ...) are highly correlated: bootstrap participation ratio of a 216-cell grid is 2.2 (noise), 3.6 (gap). Assuming N_eff 50-100 across ~25 pre-registrations, the expected best z is 2.3-2.5 (3.7 if 5,000 were independent), 95th percentile of the max ~3.0. The adopted gap cell has z 2.16 (one-sided p 0.015 normal, ~0.03 t10; Sidak over 40 / 80 looks 0.46 / 0.71); in its own grid P(max z >= 2.16 | no cell better than baseline) = 0.25 noise / 0.29 gap (White reality check, 11 year blocks, crude). The account-level PR16 gates (z > 2 and both halves positive, before placebo and farm gates) fire on a null grid in 29% / 34% of resamples. One real structure: the gap grid has 24 passes vs 2.3 expected under null (RC p 0.017, cells a2 0.4 / R 12,800) but they move the farm mean by only $1.0-1.4k. The adopted cell's gain is 49% from 2020 starts, 28% from 2017, -$27.6k from 2014; dropping 2020 takes +15.8 to +9.5 per session. **The placebo allowance is the wrong control**: placebo gain sd is 2.1-4.1 vs 14.5-15.6 real (a zero-edge stream pays almost nothing, so nothing scales); allowance $3.8 gap / $13.2 noise. Cell gains in the first vs second dev half correlate 0.19 (noise) / 0.04 (gap) across 216 cells, so in-sample ranking carries ~no out-of-sample information. Account-level walk-forward (reselect the 216-cell grid each year, 2-year embargo, 2014-21 starts): noise -$6.3/session vs fixed baseline (CI -18.5..+8.7), gap +$12.7 (CI -19.4..+39.1), each 4 of 8 years won. The incumbent is not a null: its cell ranks 50th of 216 (noise) and 141st (gap), and 3+2 was the user's layout (PR9's rule picked gap x5).
6. **Robustness checks that mislead or inform.** Leave-one-start-year-out is uninformative: the same winner survives 11 of 11 deletions (2+3 by mean; gap x5 by median, p25, positive share) because windows overlap. Drop by episode: without the 128 farms touching 2017-18, mean 3+2 -0.3k / 2+3 -0.4k / gap x5 +3.5k, median -1.8k / -1.3k / +1.6k, positive 42% / 22% / 71%. 2+3's +$5.1k mean edge is +$10.0k inside 2017-18 farms and -$0.1k outside: leverage on one episode, not extra edge. Expanding walk-forward on farm nets (decide each 1 Jan 2014-19 from farms already completed; first decisions rest on 14 and 40 farms): OOS mean minus fixed 3+2 ($22.0k) = -5.5k (pick by mean), -6.6k (median), +8.2k (p25); with the S1-gap family +8.5k / +8.8k / +9.3k. Sign flips with criterion and family; the OOS years are carried by 2016-17 starts. **Edge-scaling map** (each sleeve's dev edge scaled by lambda in {0, .5, 1}, same path): 3+2 is best by mean only when lambda_noise >= lambda_gap with gap <= half; at full edge for both, 2+3 wins ($17.9k vs $12.7k); gap full and noise half: gap x5 $11.5k vs 3+2 $0.9k. Maximum regret over the 9 scenarios, mean: 2+3 $10.1k, gap x5 $13.5k, 3+2 $18.7k; median 6.9 / 10.1 / 13.5k; p25 gap x5 3.1k best. 3+2 is never the minimax-regret split (but has the lowest average regret on median and positive share). A hedge, not evidence.

## (b) Pre-registerable protocol: is 3+2 (and its sizing) still best?
- **P0 Set.** H0 = keep 3+2 at the baseline cells (1.2 / $2,000 / 0.8 / R 9,600). Frozen candidates: 2+3 and gap x5 at the same cells (noise x5 and 4+1 dropped: -$5.2k [-8.2, -2.3]). Sizing is a separate pre-registration, one factor at a time. Two contrasts, Holm. Declared before running: primary = paired mean 3-year farm net; guards = median, p25, and the no-2017-18 stratum.
- **P1 Engine.** Calendar-year block resample of dev (13 blocks, B >= 1,000, identity draw must reproduce $12,719), real world and zero-edge world, same seeds for all arms (paired), plus the 3 x 3 edge-scaling map. Replaces the 247-overlapping-farm interval. Prototype `work/jobA.py` (180 draws, ~15 min on 3 cores).
- **P2 Accept a switch to X iff all:** (i) P_boot(X > 3+2 on mean) >= 0.975; (ii) the same >= 0.80 on median and on p25; (iii) mean gain >= +$5k per 3-year farm (+$6.6/session, ~half of MDE80); (iv) in farms not touching 2017-18, X's median and p25 not worse than 3+2 by more than $1k; (v) gain exceeds the 95th percentile of the zero-edge paired difference (structural drift: 2+3 +2.2k, gap x5 +2.7k); (vi) expanding walk-forward with the same rule, decided only on completed farms, has OOS mean >= fixed 3+2 under both mean and median criteria. Today: 2+3 0.87 / 0.79 / 0.87, gap x5 0.63 / 0.80 / 0.89, (vi) fails: CANNOT TELL.
- **P3 Deflation for any searched cell.** Account-level year-block t (not normal) with z >= 3.0 (Sidak, N_eff 50-100, one-sided 5%) and the whole procedure run on >= 200 zero-edge and half-edge resamples; use the procedure only if its false-accept rate is <= 5% (today's account gates: 29-34%). Replace the placebo allowance by the null 95th percentile of the max statistic.
- **P4 Cannot tell (state the expected result in advance: power for +$5k is ~30%, so P(cannot tell) >= 70%).** Keep 3+2 as the labelled default (no evidence for a change, no switching cost either way). Present {3+2, 2+3, gap x5} as a frozen preference menu: mean, median, p25, positive share, days to first payout (167 / 205 / 236); weight on mean favours 2+3, on the floor and positive share gap x5, on payout speed 3+2. No new dev grids; revisit only under rule R3 below.

## (c) Top-3 next steps (ranked)
1. **Sleeve-evidence audit + edge-scaling decision map.** Q: which split survives uncertainty about how real each sleeve is? Design: per-sleeve annual-block t, DSR at N = 100 / 1000, ES port, 2023-26 as soft; lambda grid 5 x 5 x 5 splits x B = 200 resampled histories; prior on lambda fixed from the DSR before running. Accept: a non-3+2 split only if its expected regret is lower by >= $3k (about one paired SD of path noise) and its maximum regret is lower; else a tie, and preference rules. Control: grid and prior declared first, no sleeve re-tuning, no cell search. Cost: ~2 CPU-hours, half a day.
2. **Procedure-level null calibration of the project's acceptance machinery.** Q: what are the false-accept rate and power of PR16-style rules? Design: run grid + gates + farm checks inside null, half-edge and real resamples (grid ~1.5 min per draw, ~1.5 h for 200 draws on 4 cores). Accept: keep a rule only if false-accept <= 5% and power >= 50% for a +40% rate change; otherwise raise its threshold to the null 95th percentile. Control: harness and seeds frozen, all draws reported. Cost: ~1 day including independent review.
3. **Forward-test statistics at sleeve level (only fresh data).** Q: what can fresh data decide, and when? SE of mean R after n years: noise 1.88e-2 / sqrt(n) vs dev mean 0.87e-2; gap 1.35e-2 / sqrt(n) vs 1.31e-2. Years for 80% power (one-sided 5%): gap 6.6 at full dev edge, 26 at half; noise 29 and 117. So the forward test can only kill, not confirm. Design: annual O'Brien-Fleming looks; kill a sleeve if cumulative mean R < 0 with one-sided p < 0.2 after >= 500 sessions; farm net informational only (75-session farms are 22% positive); promote a different split only if fresh gap t >= 2 and noise t <= 1 over >= 3 years (rule R3). Control: boundaries frozen, no interim peeking. Cost: no compute; calendar time.

## (d) What NOT to do
- No more dev grids, filters, splits or "frontier" pairs: the information per look is ~0 (half-to-half cell-gain correlation 0.04-0.19; walk-forward reselection no better than fixed) while the look count keeps rising.
- Do not treat 247 overlapping farms as 247 observations, quote farm-mean differences below ~$5k, or rank by farm mean (128 farms touching 2017-18 supply it all).
- Do not use leave-one-start-year-out as robustness (use episodes), nor the edge-removed placebo as a significance test of the edge or as selection-noise control.
- Do not adopt the gap S1 cell (z 2.16, family-wise ~0.3 in its grid, half from 2020 starts), nor the 46,656-pair "frontier" points, nor read 2023-26 as confirmation (soft; noise edge faded in 2025-26).
- Do not read a ranking finer than the path-chaos floor: +$100 beyond the daily limit raises the baseline mean ($13.6k vs $12.7k) while +$250 cuts it to $2.1k, a $10.6k swing that exceeds every config difference here; resolve the liquidation-fill assumption (model risk) before more sampling-risk work.

## (e) Biggest thing the lead may be wrong about
That "nothing beats 3+2, so 3+2 is the overfit-free default" and the open question is farm mechanics. The burden is asymmetric: challengers must clear 2 SE, halves and a placebo, while the incumbent (a dev winner of 4,752 configs, with a user-chosen layout) is exempt. The same bootstrap that says nothing beats 3+2 says 3+2 does not beat 2+3 or gap x5 either (P ~ 0.6-0.9 on every metric the other way). The deeper issue is that the split is really a bet on sleeve identity: the sleeve holding 76% of Combine time has t 1.6 (0.6 without 2017-18), a negative ES replication and 69% of its edge in two years, while the sleeve holding 24% has t 3.5. If that is believed, the farm should lean gap; if not, 3+2 is justified only as a risk preference, not as the evidence-supported optimum.
