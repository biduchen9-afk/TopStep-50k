## Part A: cost basis (foundation fix)
`retest_cost.rebase`: cost_t(new) = cost_t(modelled) x m_k x f_t with f_t = min(1, P_t / P_ref), P_t = unadjusted NQ session close from the stored raw data, P_ref = mean of P_t over 2024-01-01 to 2026-07-02, m_k = (1.22 + 0.50 k) / 1.72 for k ticks of slippage per round trip (k = 1 is the modelled cost). The rule is fixed here and not tuned. Cost bases scored: (a) as modelled (dev costs), (b) re-based at k = 1, (c) re-based at k = 2, (d) re-based at k = 3. The unavoidable assumption is that the strategies' percentage edge does not depend on the price level; it is stated wherever a re-based number appears. Reported for each basis, for the baseline cells (a1 1.2, first payout at $2,000, a2 0.8, R 9,600) and the 3 noise + 2 gap split: account-level rate and funded-account paid share and days at the farm mix; realised 3-year farm mean, median, p10, p25, share positive, worst; cohort net (every payout to the end of the dev data of the Combines bought in the window, no purchases after it, starts with at least 252 sessions of life left) mean, median, p25; median calendar days to the farm's first payout; peak cash at risk (minimum of the cash line: median and p90) and P(cash line ever <= -$2k / -$5k / -$10k).

## Part C: rules scenarios (basis (b), baseline cells, 3+2), each against its own zero-change reference
(1) all accounts forced onto the Consistency path (40% best-day rule, 3 days, cap $6,000); (2) payouts capped at 5 and at 3 per account (Live call-up); (3) $30 fee on every payout (ACH/wire; Aeropay and Prop-to-Brokerage are free); (4) Standard-path price: $199 a month plus $149 per XFA, no discount (the daily loss limit stays on); (5) XFA ladder flat at 30 micros up to a $3,000 balance; (6) combined adverse case: basis (c), 2 ticks per contract overshoot, $30 per payout, payouts capped at 5. FOMC-window entry blocks are not scored: the dates cannot be verified offline; CPI and NFP windows (08:25 to 08:35 ET) fall before the 09:30 ET entry start. The cross-account opposite-position check is reported as measured (see above).

## Part D: the frozen split menu (statistician's protocol), basis (b)
Candidates: 3 noise + 2 gap (incumbent), 2 noise + 3 gap, 5 gap; baseline cells for both groups. The audit-selected gap cell is not used (not adopted by the user). Evaluation: (i) the PR16 account and farm metrics and cohort net, paired by start year-block, year-block bootstrap intervals; (ii) **regime-resampled bootstrap**: B = 200 synthetic histories built by drawing the 12 calendar years 2011 to 2022 with replacement (the same year draw for both sleeves), farms every 20 sessions, each of the three splits run on each history; P(candidate beats 3+2) on the farm mean, median and p25 is the share of histories with a positive difference; (iii) the same with the edge removed (placebo streams) to measure how often each rule below fires on a world with no edge; (iv) worst case across the fill stress {0, 2, 4} ticks per contract.
**Switch rule (all required):** P(beats) >= 0.975 on the mean and >= 0.80 on the median and on p25; the cohort-mean gain on the real dev farms is at least $5,000; no worse than -$1,000 (cohort median) in the stratum of starts outside 2016 to 2018; the real gain exceeds the 95th percentile of the zero-edge gain; and the candidate is not worse than 3+2 in the worst case across the fill stress. **Otherwise 3+2 stays as the labelled default and the report says "cannot tell".** The expected result stated in advance (statistician): "cannot tell" with probability >= 70%.
