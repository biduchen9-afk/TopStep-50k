# Pre-registration 15 (committed BEFORE any post-2026-07-02 data is loaded): forward test and paper trading of the frozen baseline

## What is frozen
The baseline: the three NQ micro sleeves as in `final_strategy.py` (noise-area momentum; VWAP+ATR k=0.3, s=0.2; gap-VWAP gap30), flat by 15:55 ET, entries from 09:30 ET (noise-area from 10:00, gap-VWAP from 10:00), costs $1.22 + 1 tick per micro round trip; the Topstep default farm (3 noise-area + 2 gap-VWAP funded slots, ONE Combine open at a time, Combine size round(9,600 / ATR$) micros, funded size 1.2 x cushion / ATR before the first payout (first payout at a $2,000 balance, 50%) and 0.8 after, the Topstep 150K rules as modelled in `firm_engine.topstep_150k`). The code is the repository at the commit that contains this file; the bar-by-bar engines in `paper/` are parity-tested against the back-test (`test_paper.py`). No parameter, rule, size or setting may be changed during the test; changes start a new, separately pre-registered test.

## Data and validity checks (before any result is read)
Source: the London Strategic Edge vault (`paper/lse_fetch.py`), one-minute bars of the CME E-mini / Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 front month from 2026-07-03. The test is INVALID (and is reported as invalid, not as a failure of the strategy) if any of these fails: (1) the instrument is the CME NQ/MNQ future (not an index, ETF or CFD proxy; `paper/lse_probe.py` lists what the vault has - the SDK labels its futures class 'eurex', so NQ may not exist there); (2) volume is present (zero-volume share < 5%; the sleeves use a volume-weighted VWAP); (3) the regular-hours session is complete (median >= 370 of the 385 one-minute bars from 09:30 to 15:55 ET, and at most 2% of sessions below that); (4) the session structure is 18:00 -> 16:00 ET with timestamps convertible to America/New_York without gaps at the DST changes; (5) price continuity: where the vault history overlaps the stored Databento data (the fetch should start in June 2026), minute closes agree to within the bid-ask noise after removing a constant offset; a different roll / adjustment convention is allowed only if the series is continuous across each roll inside the test window (otherwise rolls are skipped by the harness' roll guard: an 8-basis-point one-minute jump inside the quarterly roll window ends trading for that session).

## What is evaluated (all reported; only (A) and (B) carry a verdict)
(A) Strategy level. For each sleeve and for the 3 noise-area : 2 gap-VWAP pooled position (one micro each): total P&L per micro over the window, mean daily P&L, hit rate, annualised Sharpe, and the PERCENTILE of the window total among every equal-length window of the development period (2010-06 to 2022-12, repaired every-session data, `repair_splices.py`). Expectation from the development data: positive in about 2 of 3 windows of 75 sessions, with a very wide spread; a single window says little.
(B) Kill criteria, evaluated on the pooled sleeve P&L and the sleeves: stop and investigate (the strategy is "not confirmed") if (i) the data checks fail; (ii) after at least 60 sessions the pooled P&L is below the 5th percentile of development windows of the same length; (iii) after 120 sessions both sleeves have a negative cumulative P&L. A window that is merely below the median is NOT a failure; one above the 90th percentile is NOT a confirmation (sampling noise on one window is large, and the edge removed placebos lose, so the strategy needs a positive edge to pay).
(C) Farm level (descriptive only): Combines bought, passed, funded, paid, fees, payouts, net, with the percentile among development farms of the same length. Reference distribution (repaired every-session data, baseline farms started every 5 sessions, `many_starts.py`): over 75 sessions mean -$0.6k, median -$1.2k, p10 -$2.0k, p90 +$2.0k, 22% of farms positive; over 190 sessions mean +$0.5k, median -$1.4k, 41% positive; over 756 sessions (3 years, starts every 10 sessions) mean $9.3k, median $2.0k, p10 -$6.6k, p90 $36.3k, 64% positive. A farm that is net negative after 3-6 months is the expected outcome and is not evidence against the strategy.

## Cadence and stopping
The harness (`python -m paper.run --source lse ...`) is run continuously; weekly the `forward_test` report is regenerated from the stored state. The test runs for at least 120 sessions (about 6 months) before any conclusion about the farm; strategy-level statistics are re-read at 60 and 120 sessions. The audit of October 2026 (FINDINGS section 27) showed that the farm outcome is extremely path dependent; this test deliberately does not use farm net as its pass/fail measure.

## Honest limits
Paper trading assumes fills at the next bar's open with the back-test's costs; real fills in fast markets will be worse (the audit found stops fill exactly at the stop in the model; one extra tick per stop would cost 2-6% of sleeve P&L). Topstep's rules in the engine come from third-party summaries and the result is highly sensitive to several of them (FINDINGS section 27, item 4); the forward test does not resolve that. The paper account never receives a real payout.
