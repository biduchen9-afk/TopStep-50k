# Council seat: FARM / PORTFOLIO ARCHITECT (structure, capital, scaling, risk of ruin)
Method: council/fm.py is my own copy of run_multifirm's ungated path plus hooks (concurrency, bankroll, purchase gates, cash path). Net P&L is identical to the repo's `run_multifirm` on all 247 dev farms for k=1, 2, 3, 5 (max |diff| 0.0; k>=3 = Policy(global_one_eval=False, stagger_gap=5) with spec.max_evals 3/2). Repaired data, rules v2, 247 farms (starts 2010-06..2020-01, 756 sessions). Repo untouched. "Peak cash" = -min of the cumulative cash line (fee when bought, payout when received). "Cohort net" = every payout (to end-2022) of the Combines bought inside the window minus their fees, 222 farms with >=1y of tail (removes the 3-year cut-off). ~90 configurations were run, all unregistered: treat gaps under ~$5k as ties. Files: council/exp*.csv.

## 0. What the 247 farms are (changes how every number below is read)
- Integrated autocorrelation of the 247 nets: N_eff ~ 5.5 (about 3 independent 3-year windows). - Window effect: the cut-off drops payouts of live accounts. Baseline on the same 222 farms: realised mean $13.0k / median $2.2k, cohort $31.7k / $7.7k.

- Standing preference (oop only to the first payout, one pipeline, then self-funded, reserve 3 cycles; repo Policy(k0=1, reserve_cycles=3, global_one_eval, group_aware, single_pipeline)): out-of-pocket median $1.4k, p90 $3.6k, max $5.4k (39% need > $2k, 0.8% > $5k); the seed is exhausted (nothing alive, cash < $199) in 64% of 3-year farms (93% of 2010-14 starts, 38% of 2015+); net mean $8.9k, median -$0.8k, 36% positive, worst -$5.4k; 26 Combines instead of 55. ## (c) Comparing configurations without overfitting
1. Unit = start year (11 blocks), not 247 farms; same starts for all configs (paired); report year-block CI and a sign count by start year; require non-inferior in 2010-14 starts and better in 2015-19.
2. Score cohort net (payouts of all accounts bought in the window), not the 3-year cut-off, or slow payers lose by construction (signs flip above).
3. Metric order: cohort median, cohort p25, P(peak cash >= $10k), trimmed mean (10%); the raw mean last (top 10% of farms = 54% of the sum; 3 regimes).
4. Compare at equal cash at risk: mean/p90 peak cash (k=1 1.2, k=5 1.8, 10 slots 2.1) or within +-20% of the same peak p90; leverage moves mean and loss together.
5. Stress-gate every candidate: DLL slip $0/$100/$250, price $229, activation +1 session; rank on the minimum over the set (3+2 falls to $2.1k at $250, gap-heavy mixes do not).
6. Declare <= 3 candidates; log all looks (90 here, 185+ before); placebo allowance; tie if |diff| < $5k (N_eff 5.5).

## (d) Ranked recommendations (pre-registrable)
1. Seed-capped live pilot under the standing preference. Design: baseline cells, the policy above, seed $5k, hard stop at the seed, no re-injection without a new decision. Primary readouts are measurements, not net: DLL liquidation overshoot per event, Combine pass rate (model 21% noise / 29% gap), activation lag, payout days, days to first payout. Pre-declare: overshoot >= $150 means the baseline mean is under $5k, so switch to a gap-heavy mix or stop; below $100 continue.
2. Payout-financed concurrency (the pyramid row). One look against k=1 on the 247 starts and the 4 seen starts. Pass if cohort median >= 1.5x baseline, cohort p25 not lower, P(cash <= -$10k) <= baseline +5 points (observed 17% vs 12%, at the edge), 2010-14 starts not worse by more than $1k, and slip $250 mean > 0. Only starts after the pilot has paid out $3k.
3. Split chosen by stress-minimum, not mean. Candidates 3+2, 2+3 (gap = S1 winner), gap x5, at k=1 and slip 0/100/250, plus the 2023 and 2025 seen starts. Rule: highest worst-case cohort median subject to p90 peak cash <= $10k. Observed cohort median 3+2 7.7/7.5/-4.0, 2+3(S1) 21.2/9.3/6.7, gap x5 12.5/12.2/12.0 (slip 0/100/250); gap x5 fails the 2023 seen start (-8.3), 3+2 fails slip 250.

## (e) What NOT to do
- Do not raise concurrency (k=5, 7-10 slots, parallel firms) to chase the mean or the 90-day goal: it multiplies the same regime exposure (worst -21.8/-34.0/-42.2) and breaks the one-Combine preference.
- Do not add permanent kill rules tuned on dev (they cost 25-58% of cohort mean) or sleeve-trailing-P&L gates, consistency path, 30% withdrawals, t1/t2 tuning, cool-off restarts.
- Do not quote 60% positive, p25 or "mature $22k" as probabilities or expectations; do not pick on a farm mean or on a 3-year cut-off.
- Do not count FTMO/FundedNext as diversification (corr 0.86/0.74, rules from snippets); do not top up the seed after it is gone without a new decision.

## (f) The single biggest thing the lead may be wrong about
FINDINGS 28 reads the 247-farm table as a risk distribution and concludes the strategy streams, not the farm mechanics, are the binding constraint. The table is about 5 draws of one regime switch (0 of 76 positive for 2012-14 starts, 121 of 129 for 2015-19, 99% of dollars from 2016-18 starts), so "60% positive" and "p25 -$4.7k" are not probabilities, and the capital to hold is the bad-regime figure ($8-12k now, uncapped beyond 3 years), not the p25. And concurrency, which was tested only at 2, changes the dev mean 2.4x and the median 6x (k=5) at 1.6x the cash risk: farm mechanics scale the regime bet by a lever the user controls (seen 2025 starts lose 2.2x more at k=5), and 3-year accounting hides back-loaded payouts (cohort $31.7k vs realised $13.0k).
