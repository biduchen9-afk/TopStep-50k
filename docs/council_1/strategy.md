# Strategy / edge seat: where the sleeves earn, what to research next
Data: repaired every-session NQ (`data_raw_live`); R = sleeve P&L / ATR14 dollars per micro (what the farm sizes on); dev = 2011-2022, seen = 2023-26H1. Code and outputs: `scratchpad/council/{a2..a10,recost,pscale,rescore,esport,tail,short,recent}.py`, `strat_*.out|csv`. Everything below is measurement, nothing was selected.
**Bottom line.** The "regime dependence" of the sleeves is mostly (i) a cost-per-ATR drift in the dev sample and (ii) the farm's convexity turning a stationary edge into a lumpy start-year table. The gross edge looks stable; the real open risk is decay in 2025-26 (not significant), not volatility regimes.

## (a) Diagnosis
1. **Costs, not regimes, made 2011-2016 look flat.** The model charges a fixed $1.72 per micro round trip = 0.86 NQ points. As % of ATR14 (35-65 pts then, 400-500 pts now): 2.5% (2013), 1.3% (2015-16), 0.35% (2020-24), 0.2% (2025-26). nq_noise Sharpe gross/net: dev 1.28/0.62, seen 1.31/1.16; nq_gap30: dev 1.39/0.98, seen 0.92/0.82. Costs eat 51% / 29% of gross edge in dev, 11% / 12% in 2023+. Yearly net Sharpe vs cost-in-R: r = -0.60 (noise). Yearly gross Sharpe SD is 0.83 (noise) / 0.80 (gap30) around 1.3 / 1.2.
2. **Re-costed dev farms** (cost x min(1, P_t/22,119), i.e. same % moves at today's price; `recost.py`, 247 farms, baseline 3+2 split): mean $32.3k / median $21.4k / p25 $14.6k / p10 $8.0k / 95% positive / worst -$3.2k, against $12.7k / $2.9k / -$4.7k / -$8.5k / 60% / -$11.6k as modelled. Full price-scaling (also removes the 150-micro cap and fine granularity; ~15 micros per Combine instead of 76; `pscale.py`): $52.3k / $41.1k / p25 $15.0k / 92% positive. Seen starts (real, cheap-cost era): 2021 $48.6k, 2023 $25.9k, 2025-01 -$6.8k. Treat re-costed numbers as a counterfactual re-measurement, not a forecast (needs the % edge to be price-independent).
3. **Start-year concentration is what luck produces.** Shuffle the dev days of each stream (same R mean, same ATR path, no regime; 200 shuffles, `a7.py`) and re-run the account evaluator: SD of start-year rates null median 45.8 vs observed 58.0 (P = 0.43 day-shuffle, 0.20 63-day blocks) for noise; 77.8 vs 87.2 (P = 0.43) for gap30; best-year rate P = 0.39 / 0.45; top-2-year share P = 0.29 / 0.24; negative start-years null median 6 vs 5 observed. So 2017-18 dominance and 2013-16 weakness carry no evidence of regime dependence.
4. **No persistence to exploit.** Variance ratios of daily R at 21-252 days: noise 0.87-0.97 (p 0.4-0.75), vwap 0.64-0.81, gap30 0.77-0.97 (all <= 1; >1 would mean regimes). Quarterly gross-R lag-1 autocorrelation -0.19 (noise) / -0.15 (gap30). Intraday 30-min return autocorrelation vs quarterly sleeve R: r = 0.00 contemporaneous, 0.21 predictive (n = 61 quarters, ns). Calendar-year net Sharpe SD 1.03 (noise) vs ~1.0 expected under constant Sharpe (chi2 14.7 / 14 df).
5. **Volatility terciles (causal, ATR% / its 250-day mean), Sharpe low|mid|high.** Net dev: noise -0.13|0.85|1.51, gap30 0.25|1.12|1.81. Gross: noise 0.60|1.54|1.95 (dev) but 1.32|1.01|1.69 (seen); gap30 0.72|1.54|2.05 and 0.59|0.93|1.44. High beats low in 8/13 (noise) and 10/13 (gap30) years. Half the net gradient is cost; the gross gradient is moderate and weak in seen data. Trailing 20-day vol and ATR% level give no cleaner pattern.
6. **What does drive P&L: ~21 big trend days a year.** Days with |open-to-close| > 1 ATR are 8.1% of days (325 in 15.5 y); they carry 193% (noise) / 131% (gap30) of gross profit (other days: -0.024 R/day for noise). Yearly share of such days vs yearly gross R: r = 0.55 / 0.52 (n = 15). Sleeve captures 42% (noise) / 20% (gap30) of the available |move| on those days. Independent bets per year are ~20 trend days, not 154 / 82 trading days.
7. **Sleeve overlap.** Daily R correlations: noise-vwap 0.70, noise-gap30 0.60 (0.85 on days both trade; monthly 0.61), noise-ES 0.64, GC ~0.02. Effective N = 1.68 for the three NQ sleeves, 2.73 with ES and GC. The farm is essentially one bet.
8. **Calendar/event days: nothing usable.** Weekday Sharpe (noise, Mon-Fri) 0.8/1.3/1.1/0.8/1.7, SE ~1.1. 08:30-release-shock days (11% of days, known at 09:30): Sharpe 1.71/1.50 vs 1.07/1.16, weak. FOMC-like days (14:00 5-min range > 6x median, 44 days): noise -0.087 R (t -1.4), gap30 -0.061 R (t -2.4) vs +0.02: real but only ~3 days a year; fits the Tier-1 news rule, worth ~4% of profit.
9. **Recent weakness is unresolved.** 50/50 noise+gap30 re-costed Sharpe over the last 378 sessions (from 2025-01-16): 0.43, the weakest of 10 non-overlapping 18-month windows (others 0.71-2.39), 6.7th percentile of rolling windows, z = -1.1 against the full-sample mean. 18-month farm starts (re-costed dev windows: mean $11.1k, median $7.6k, 74% positive, worst -$7.3k): real 2025-01 start -$6.8k is at the worst edge; 2021-24 starts +$3.3k / +$17.4k / +$8.0k / +$14.5k. SE of an 18-month Sharpe is ~0.8, so decay and bad luck cannot be separated yet.

## (b) New research directions (all intraday, entries >= 09:30, flat 15:55, micros)
**D1. Re-base the dev evaluation at today's cost-to-ATR, then re-score what was rejected.** Basis: audit-style foundation fix. Helps: removes the artefact that made 2011-16 "flat" and biased every filter/sizing/ES-GC verdict (the old grids optimised against a 2.5%-of-ATR cost). Evidence it matters: the 94 re-scorable ES/GC trials (sections 16-17; 10 pre-09:30 COMEX-ORB trials refuse to run) go from median dev Sharpe -0.61 to -0.23, and 0 -> 4 reach 0.5 (all four are ES gap30 ports, 0.63-0.73; ES noise-area 0.46; best-of-94 noise bar ~0.65-0.7, so still not accepted). Candidate list: 0 new configs; re-run the existing 455 (PR16) plus 94 ES/GC trials on price-scaled streams; count as one extra look at each. Sample: same 12.5 dev years. Control: fix the scaling rule before running (P_ref = 2024-26 mean price), report cost at 1/2/3 ticks, apply all PR16 acceptance rules unchanged.
**D2. Independent-sample validation of the two frozen sleeves (zero parameters).** Basis: Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon SSRN 4824172 report Sharpe 1.33 on SPY 2007-2024 (own backtest, no independent replication found; a June 2026 SSRN replication reports 0.39 out of sample, per FINDINGS 17, unverified); Gao et al. JFE 2018 (volatile/news days stronger; effect found in 12 of 16 equity markets). Helps: answers whether gross Sharpe ~1.2 is real across regimes. Data to buy (not in repo): 2000-2010 intraday for QQQ/SPY (or NDX/SPX futures if a vendor has them; cost model uncertain, show 3 cost levels) and 2010+ RTY/YM. Candidates (10): {noise-area, gap30 VWAP at k=0.3, s=0.2} x {QQQ 2000-10, SPY 2000-10, RTY, YM, ES}; sign-flipped placebos not counted. Sample: +10 years incl. 2000-02 bear and 2008 (about doubles independent years and big-move days: ~210 more). Control: pass = Sharpe > 0 and positive in >= 60% of years per instrument; no re-tuning, no ranking among instruments.
**D3. Trend-day capture and a finer decision grid at today's costs.** Basis: Zarattini et al. (trailing stop, "unlimited upside"); my measurement 6 (8% of days = 193% of profit, 42% / 20% capture). Helps: attacks the volatility dependence directly by monetising the rare large days harder, using same-day information only (no regime forecast); farm payoff is convex. Candidates (12): noise-area decision step {10, 15, 30 min} x add-on {none, +1 unit once open profit >= 0.75 ATR, >= 1.25 ATR} (9); gap30 add-on {none, 0.75, 1.25 ATR} (3). Sample: ~260 big days in dev, ~75 seen; thin. Control: evaluate on R series at today's cost, random-timing add-on placebo, require gain in both dev halves and in 2023+, deflate for 12 + prior VWAP looks (156).
**D4. Uncorrelated assets via frozen ports (needs data).** Basis: Gao et al. effect appears in non-equity futures but evidence for crude, FX and Treasuries is thin (search found only passing mentions); scheduled releases (EIA Wed 10:30, ISM/consumer confidence 10:00) fall after 09:30. Helps: only true independent bets (GC is 0.02 correlated with NQ sleeves but its re-costed Sharpe is 0.16 dev / 0.77 seen). Caution: Sharpe adds in quadrature: re-costed ES ports (noise 0.44 dev / 0.56 seen, gap30 0.60 / 0.41, corr 0.4-0.65 with NQ) LOWER the equal-risk NQ portfolio Sharpe from 1.36 to 1.07 (dev); at the optimal weight they add ~0.02. A new leg needs Sharpe >= ~0.8 to matter. Candidates (6): noise-area and gap30 ported unchanged to MCL, 6E, ZN. Control: as D2; expect failure and stop after one run.
**D5. NQ-ES intraday spread (watch list only).** Basis: first-30-min spread momentum, corr to the rest of the day 0.069 (t = 4.4; dev 0.086, 2023+ -0.001; positive in 14/17 years); gross ~1.9 bps/day on dev and 0.3 seen, vs ~1.1 bps round-trip cost (MNQ + 2 MES); one of three correlations scanned. Net Sharpe <= ~0.3, decayed, rules question (two-product hedge inside one account) for the prop-firm seat. Candidates (3): follow first-30 spread move to 15:55 / to 12:00 / only top-30% |move| days. Not recommended until D1-D3 are done.

## (c) Ranking
1. **D1** (cost-normalised foundation, 0 new configs): largest effect found (dev mean +$20-40k, median +$18-38k), and it changes what every earlier negative verdict means.
2. **D2** (independent years/instruments, 10 configs): the only item that adds real independent evidence about the edge and about 2025-26; blocks selection bias by construction.
3. **D3** (trend-day capture, 12 configs): targets the actual profit mechanism; moderate overfit risk, so only after D1 fixes the metric.

## (d) Do not
- Do not hunt volatility/trend/ADX/VR gates or trailing-performance on-off switches: VR <= 1, quarterly autocorrelation -0.19, intraday autocorrelation r = 0.00, gradient half cost; 144 + 455 looks already found nothing.
- Do not read the start-year rate table or the 2017-18 share as regime evidence (shuffle null P = 0.2-0.45); judge on medians and cost-normalised farms.
- Do not add more NQ variants of the same family (effective N 1.7), nor more sizing/trigger/R grids (185 + 455 looks; farm gains below the ~$4-6k path noise).
- Do not add weekday or macro-release filters (SE ~1.1 per cell). FOMC afternoons only as a rules matter, not an edge.
- Do not add ES ports for diversification (lowers portfolio Sharpe); GC and ES trial lists need only the D1 re-score, not new families.

## (e) The biggest thing the lead may be wrong about
That the weak 2011-16 outcomes and the 2017-18 concentration show a volatility-regime edge. They show a fixed-dollar cost against a 6-12x smaller ATR (gross Sharpe is 1.28 dev / 1.31 seen for noise) plus ordinary luck in a convex farm. Consequence: the baseline farm is likely worth far more than $12.7k / $2.9k under today's cost-to-ATR ($32k / $21k re-costed), the search spaces and verdicts built on dev data are biased, and the unresolved risk is decay (last 18 months Sharpe 0.43 vs ~1.2), which only fresh independent data (D2, forward test) can settle. Caveat to my own finding: the counterfactual assumes the % edge is price-independent and 1-tick slippage still holds at NQ 30,000; both need a check.
